Privacy Policy

Levance doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app sends nothing over the internet.

Effective 9 November 2026. Levance is made by Hao Huang.

What Levance collects

Nothing. Levance has no servers, and it doesn't send your pictures, your location or anything else to the developer or to anyone else. It contains no third-party code: no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.

What the app uses on your phone

Camera

Levance uses the camera to take pictures while its camera screen is open. Pictures are developed on your phone.

Location (optional)

Off unless you turn it on in the app's Settings. When it's on, Levance reads your location only while the camera screen is open, and writes it into the frames you take, so each frame keeps where it was taken. It goes no further than those frames and the pictures you save or share from them.

Photos

When you save a frame, Levance adds it to your photo library, in an album called Levance. To keep that album, it asks for access to your library, and uses it only to find and add to its own album. It doesn't read, copy or analyse your other photos. If you allow adding photos only, frames are still saved, just not in the album.

The details in your pictures

Frames you save or share carry details in their metadata, as if logged on film: the film stock and its speed, the date and time, the shutter speed, aperture and lens, and, if location is on, where they were taken. You can see these in the Photos app's info panel. When you share a frame, the app or service you share it with receives the picture with these details, under its own privacy policy.

Where your rolls are kept

Your rolls and frames are stored inside the app on your phone, and are included in your phone's backups (to iCloud or a computer) if you back up. Deleting the app deletes them from your phone; frames you've saved to Photos stay there.

Children

Levance collects no information from anyone, including children.
